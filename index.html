HOW TO PLAY
Desktop: WASD moves · E interacts · SPACE uses Adrenaline / hits skill checks · F uses Flashbang · LEFT SHIFT dashes · hold B to hold breath · H hides near a cabinet · N throws a Noise Maker.
Phone: Joystick moves · E interacts · ◎ Abilities · ▣ Items · HIT Skill Check when repairing. Install from the Home Screen for the cleanest fullscreen experience.
Goal: Repair every generator, then catch the fleeing monster.
GAME MODES
Challenge: Choose one of three rotating altered contracts. Each has a map rule, focused objective, and listed token reward.
Endless: Each win immediately begins the next round. Generators, speed, mutations, outages, false generators, and monster count increase; round 4 introduces a second monster.
Survival: Choose the map, monster roster, difficulty, generators, event setting, and a saved loadout. Every monster uses its core chase behavior on every map; home-map mechanics remain special.
CALEB — THE DISRUPTOR
Caleb patrols normally and periodically releases an EMP that makes the map difficult to read.
Counter: Keep moving after the warning and use landmarks to reorient yourself.
MALAKAI — THE BLOOD HUNTER
Fast and aggressive. Repairs, failed skill checks, false generators, and other mistakes can give him your last known trail for a short time.
Counter: Crouch, break line of sight, use a Noise Maker, or let a trap slow him down.
JORDAN — THE MIMIC
Can disguise himself as a generator. Interacting with him reveals the trick and eventually triggers an enraged chase.
Counter: Watch for the suspicious visual offset and be ready to run after checking a generator.
AESON — THE FIRESTARTER
Ignites circular zones that slow you and alert him to heat on every level. In the Boilerworks he also controls the central boiler.
Counter: Keep moving, use cold routes, and watch the heat meter.
BASSAM — THE CONCIERGE
Disguises himself as a hotel employee and offers believable but dangerous directions. Outside the Hotel, he sends false employees after you every 10–20 seconds.
Counter: Check badges, shadows, and directions before trusting an employee. False employees can be outrun or stunned with a well-timed escape.
RHYS — THE CONTAINED
Spits short-range goop that slows you, creates slowing puddles, and charges in a straight line after a brief brace on every level. On Crimson Containment, restore the generators before recovering the seal and arming the vault trap.
Counter: Dodge sideways during the brace, use the Goop Neutralizer, and bait his special charge into the cracked wall when that route is active.
NOAH — THE STALKER
Invisible for most of the run, Noah closes in nearby, briefly reveals himself, then makes a short intercept burst before fading back into the forest. He can call lightning circles that heavily slow and blur you.
Counter: Stay in lit cabins, break line of sight, move out of lightning circles, and use a Flashbang to scatter him. Audio hooks are intentionally ready but silent until custom files are added.
AMINE — THE PARTED
Invisible normally. Hold Focus Vision to hide the map and reveal only Amine. He flashes periodically and teleports behind you. His Parted Grid chase is unique to his home map.
Counter: Alternate normal sight and Focus Vision, keep moving after a teleport, and reach the exit gate for the exposed final chase.
NIZAR — THE CONTROLLER
Uses signal distortion on every level. His Closing Frames appear on both sides and push inward; move before they pin you. He can also split into two False Nizars that chase you without a shadow. The real Nizar always casts one. The Last Line control chase is unique to the Subway.
Counter: Restore signal panels, keep moving when the frames appear, and use the real shadow to identify the hunter.
LUCAS — THE SHADOW AT THE GATE
At the Shadow Gate, Lucas seals routes, fires shadow arrows, pulls you toward him, and can emerge from a nearby shadow pool. Recover and carry the fragments to the Caleb Vessel to finish the encounter.
Counter: Keep moving when the route seals or arrows appear, and resist each fragment pull before it rises beyond reach.
MUTATIONS
Speed Demon: Flat speed boost.
Frenzy: Gains a speed boost with every repaired generator.
Giant: Massive visual size.
Camouflage: Color blends perfectly into the floor.
Phantom: Glides slowly through solid walls.
All-Seeing: Periodically tracks your exact location.
Gloom: Significantly darkens the map's shadows.
Reinforced: Generators require an extra interaction to fix.
Lethargy: Player base running speed is reduced by 10%.
Resilient: Flashbang stun duration is halved.
Scrambler: Hides your generator progress UI.
Hexed: Player boosts (Adrenaline) only last half as long.
Hallucinations: Creates harmless visual decoys nearby. Do not chase every shape you see.
Locked In: Can seal a nearby doorway for longer.
Echo: False sound rings can appear away from the real danger.
False Objective: Periodically corrupts the objective text on your HUD.
Watcher: Camera and mirror routes reveal your position more often.
Panic: Skill-check zones become less stable while the monster is close.
Heavy Footfall: The monster is slower but its search pressure lasts longer.
Afterimage: Leaves a harmless delayed silhouette when it changes direction.
They can also fake generator markers, alerts, and HUD progress. They never deal damage.
PROGRESSION
Cosmetics: Colors, trails, hats, masks, and body skins are cosmetic-only rewards for Hard wins, monster catches, Endless progress, special challenges, and long-term career milestones. Equip skins from the SKINS tab in the Collector’s Book; image previews appear there.
Statistics: The Statistics screen records wins, losses, generators, catches, Endless best, fastest win, item use, and favorite monster.
Inventory: You can carry up to eight consumables. Ground supplies only fit when a slot is free. Custom loadouts choose exact item quantities, up to eight total; Free Carry permits any owned supply.
Specialist Gear: Signal Scramblers block tracking alerts with X, Goop Neutralizers clear slowing hazards, Repair Kits widen the next skill-check zone, and Flares brighten the area but reveal your location. The permanent Dash Module uses LEFT SHIFT and has a five-second cooldown.
TIPS
- Use 'E' to repair generators.
- Skill check needles now loop endlessly! Take your time to hit the green zone.
- Jordan has a 30-second cooldown before he can sabotage multiple generators.
THE LOBBY — LEVEL 0
Objective: Repair every generator, then catch the fleeing monster.
Field note: Level 0 is the starting location and the baseline for the shift. Use the generator, hiding, and safety guidance in the field notes while you learn its routes.
MAP EVENTS & GENERATORS
Power Outage: The map darkens for a short time. Maintenance rooms can restore the lights early.
Emergency Lights: Red alarms reveal both locations and let the monster track you through the maze too.
False Generators: Look closely for their small offset amber status light. Do not repair them.
Fuse Generator: Find the loose fuses around the map before repairing it.
Multi-stage Generator: Complete several repair stages before it comes online. It uses a circuit puzzle.
Response Array: Click each target before it expires. It is deliberately click/touch-only; missing one fails the repair and stuns you.
Color Memory: Watch the red, blue, green, and yellow sequence, then repeat it. A wrong choice fails the repair and stuns you.
Shift Cache: A rare lucky block that can contain an item, repair a generator, create another generator, or grant a temporary speed surge. Items still require free inventory space.
Safe Room: The monster cannot detect or catch you while you are inside, but it keeps moving through the map.
Hiding Spot: Hide for up to 20 seconds while the monster keeps patrolling. If it sees you enter, it will check the spot and catch you.
Crouch: Hold the crouch button to slow yourself and the monster while you reposition.
Hold Breath: Hold your breath briefly to avoid the All-Seeing mutation. It has a cooldown.
Noise Maker: Throw a sound somewhere else to pull the monster away. Completed generators also make noise.
Bear Trap: Press T or use Items to place one at your feet. It briefly stuns a monster, then disappears. Only two can be active.
THE BOILERWORKS
Objective: Repair every generator, activate all three cooling valves, then find and shut down the central boiler.
Heat: Burning sections build heat and slightly slow you. Leave the area to cool down. The Boilerworks uses long procedural halls and rooms, so routes change every run.
Monsters: Aeson is most likely here, but Jordan, Caleb, Malakai, and rare two-monster runs are possible.
THE ENDLESS HOTEL
Objective: Repair the hotel generators, complete and report staff assignments, evacuate the employees through the elevator, then catch Bassam.
Employees: Speak to staff to accept up to two assignments. Complete the work, then return to the same staff member to report it.
Events: Hotel Lockdown seals a whole corridor section temporarily. Bassam can impersonate staff and place a false assignment among your real ones. Dialogue and employee tasks pause the hunt; hiding remains usable even while Bassam or multiple monsters are active.
THE CRIMSON CONTAINMENT
Objective: Repair generators, locate the Crimson Seal through a changing route, then lure Rhys into the containment trap.
Events: Containment alarms, pressure releases, a sweeping emergency-light circle, and seal resonance. This level does not use power outages.
THE BLACKWOOD FOREST
Objective: Repair remote generators, restore two dark cabin breakers, recover the Ranger Beacon battery, then install it at the watchtower to expose Noah. Lit cabins are safe zones because Noah cannot stalk through strong light.
Events: Fog Roll darkens the woods for a short time. Layout: Procedural clearings, woodland trails, tree barriers, and cabin interiors change each run. Flashbangs scatter Noah to a distant unlit location.
THE PARTED GRID
Objective: Repair every generator, find the exit gate, then pursue Amine in the turret car. Steer around roadblocks and fire carefully—eight repetitive shots jam the turret, and Amine can vanish from the road for short periods.
Hazards: Falling into random sinkholes is fatal. Burning zones heavily slow and blur you. Before the final pursuit, Amine flashes into view and teleports nearby.
THE LAST LINE
Objective: Restore the signal panels, then reach the control room. From there, route one of several trains through the moving hunter's track within two minutes by opening and closing lane switches. The hunter can dodge between tracks, so watch the whole board and adapt.
Features: Each run assembles three or four stations along a readable connected spine, with two-track tunnels, ticket booths, service rooms, stopped cars, moving trains, and different objective locations. Route Boards are tap/click puzzles that rotate track pieces into one connected line.
Subway rewards: Earn the Stop Sign Mask first by recovering and delivering the torn ticket before restoring any signal. Then earn the Headlight by letting three trains miss Nizar, allowing the passenger train to pass while he is alive, and catching him. Finally, after both are earned, recover the gold fare sphere as Nizar before restoring any signal to unlock the Train Skin through the Gold Fare cutscene.
Gold pressure: The sphere broadcasts your position for five seconds every 25 seconds, drains outside lit stations, starts a three-minute validation timer, flickers a completed signal every 25 seconds, locks station doors, and accelerates passing trains. Re-stabilize flickering signals before Rail Control will accept the fare.
THE SHADOW GATE
Objective: Recover the first Caleb fragment, activate the three shadow relays, recover the released second fragment, then carry the final fragment to the Caleb Vessel at the sealed gate.
Fragment pull: Every fragment opens a touch/mouse challenge. Hold or drag below it to pull it back down before the shadow takes it upward.
Encounter: Lucas can seal routes, fire shadow arrows, pull you toward him, and emerge from a shadow pool near you. After the fragments are placed, step into his sight: a tether comes out of the gate and drags him into the darkness while the round resolves. Caleb's boss arena is reserved for a later patch.
MAP INTEL
Clear a map on Easy, Normal, and Hard, then buy its Map Intel in the Shop. Press M on desktop or tap MAP on mobile to open the full layout without pausing the danger.
MOBILE CONTROLS
Joystick: Move freely in any direction.
E: Interact with generators, fuses, rooms, and hiding spots.
◎ Abilities: Pauses the game and opens Crouch, Hold Breath, and Hide. Crouch is a toggle on phone.
▣ Items: Pauses the game and opens every carried supply, including Specialist Gear.
Skill Check: Appears during timing-based repairs. Tap it when the needle is inside the green zone.
OBJECTIVES & LOADOUTS
Daily Objectives: Complete rotating local objectives for bonus tokens from the main menu.
Loadouts: Build a custom kit from owned supplies, choosing exact quantities up to the eight-slot carry limit. Items are still consumed normally when used.
